Support

Questions, a fax that didn't go through, or a billing problem: email support@faxbeamapp.com. Include the date and the number you faxed so we can find it quickly.

Cancel a subscription

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose FaxBeam. Cancelling there stops future charges.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
